The Lab

$uFRINK launches through a bonding curve. Everything happens on-chain from the first block.

Buy = Mint

Every time you buy one full $uFRINK token on the curve, a unique specimen is automatically minted to your wallet in the same transaction. Fractions don’t mint. Only whole tokens do.

On-Chain Storage

All trait layers live inside the contract itself. They are written in fixed-size chunks and locked with a permanent hash. Any specimen can be rendered directly from the chain data — forever.

Fair Launch

No allowlist. No premine. Per-wallet and per-buy limits start low and slowly rise every second until they disappear completely. Everyone starts equal.

Graduation

When the curve hits its target, anyone can trigger graduation. Liquidity moves to a pool and the LP key is burned forever. Trading continues with a simple fee structure.

Fees & Buybacks

1% trading fee after launch: majority goes to daily on-chain buybacks of $uFRINK. Everything else (mint, reveal, listing) is gas only.

Marketplace

Every specimen can be listed and traded on the built-in on-chain marketplace, priced in native token, straight from the contract.
